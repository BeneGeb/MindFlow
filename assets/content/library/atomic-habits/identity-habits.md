# Identity-Based Habits

From *Atomic Habits* by James Clear

---

## The Shift That Changes Everything
Most people focus on outcomes: *"I want to lose weight."* *"I want to meditate more."*

James Clear argues there's a deeper lever: **identity**.

Instead of asking *"What do I want to achieve?"* ask *"Who do I want to become?"*

- Not *"I want to meditate"* → but *"I am someone who takes care of their mind."*
- Not *"I want to exercise"* → but *"I am someone who moves their body every day."*
- Not *"I want less stress"* → but *"I am someone who responds to stress with intention."*

---

## Why Identity Works
Every action you take is a vote for the type of person you believe you are.

When you meditate today, you cast a vote for the identity of someone who meditates. When you skip it without guilt, you cast a vote against it. The goal is not perfection — it's a consistent majority of votes in the direction you want to go.

Your habits don't change your outcomes first. They change your self-image first. And your self-image changes your future behavior automatically.

---

## How to Use It

1. **Decide who you want to be** — not what you want, but who
2. **Prove it with small wins** — every completed habit is evidence
3. **Say it quietly to yourself** — "I'm someone who meditates" after you do it
4. **Let your streaks reinforce it** — each day in a row is a stronger argument

---

## The MindFlow Connection
When MindFlow shows your streak, it's not just a number. It's a count of the votes you've cast for who you're becoming. Three days in a row isn't just three check marks — it's proof you're building a new identity.

The question isn't "did I do the habit today?" It's "did I show up as the person I'm becoming today?"

---

## A Note on Setbacks
Missing a day doesn't erase your identity. One missed vote doesn't change the election. The key is to never miss twice — and to return without shame. Identities are built over months, not broken in a day.
