# Meditation

## What it is

Meditation is the practice of training your attention and awareness to achieve a calm, clear mental state. Even 5 minutes a day creates measurable changes in your brain over time.

## Why it works

Regular meditation lowers cortisol (your stress hormone), strengthens the prefrontal cortex responsible for focus and decision-making, and improves sleep quality. Studies show that just 8 weeks of consistent practice can physically change the structure of your brain.

## How to start

1. Find a quiet spot and sit comfortably
2. Set a timer — start with just 5 minutes
3. Close your eyes and focus on your breath
4. When your mind wanders (it will), gently bring it back — no judgment
5. Mornings work best: it sets a calm tone for the entire day

## Techniques to try

- **Breath Awareness** — simply observe the sensation of breathing
- **Body Scan** — slowly move attention from head to toe
- **Counting** — count each exhale from 1 to 10, then restart

## 2-Minute Version

Sit still, close your eyes, take 3 deep breaths. That's it. On tough days, this counts.

*Use this version when you're just getting started. Once the habit feels natural, gradually increase the time.*
