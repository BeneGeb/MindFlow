# Journaling

## What it is
Journaling is the practice of writing down your thoughts, feelings, and experiences. It turns the noisy, chaotic stream of your inner world into something concrete — and that shift alone is powerful.

## Why it works
Writing activates the analytical left brain, which calms the emotional right brain. Research shows journaling reduces anxiety, improves emotional processing, boosts self-awareness, and even strengthens your immune system. It's therapy you give yourself.

## How to start
1. Choose a consistent time — morning or evening work well
2. Start with just one sentence if you're stuck
3. Don't worry about grammar or making it "good"
4. Write whatever comes — there are no rules
5. Re-reading old entries is optional but often insightful

## Prompts to try
- *What's one thing I'm grateful for today?*
- *What's weighing on me right now?*
- *What would make tomorrow feel like a win?*
- *What did I learn about myself this week?*

## 2-Minute Version
Write one sentence: what are you feeling right now? That's a journal entry.

*Use this version when you're just getting started. Once the habit feels natural, gradually increase the time.*
