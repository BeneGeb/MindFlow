# Digital Detox

## What it is
A digital detox means intentionally stepping away from screens — phones, social media, news feeds — for a set period. It doesn't require a weekend in the woods. Even 30 minutes counts.

## Why it works
The average person checks their phone 96 times per day. Every notification triggers a small stress response. Social media is designed to trigger social comparison, which consistently lowers mood and self-esteem. A detox isn't about rejecting technology — it's about reclaiming control of your attention.

## How to practice it

### Daily Mini Detox
- First 30 minutes of the morning: no phone
- Last 30 minutes before bed: no phone
- One hour of focused, screen-free time each afternoon

### Longer Detox
- One screen-free evening per week
- One full day without social media per week
- A weekend offline every month if possible

## What to do instead
- Go for a walk (notice how different it feels without earbuds)
- Cook a meal with full attention
- Read a physical book
- Have a conversation without your phone on the table
- Sit quietly and let your mind wander — this is where creativity lives

## 2-Minute Version
Put your phone in another room and sit quietly for 2 minutes. Notice what it feels like to not reach for it. That discomfort is informative.

## Atomic Habits tip
Make phone use harder and offline activities easier. A physical book on your nightstand beats a phone on your nightstand every time. Design the environment, then let it work.
