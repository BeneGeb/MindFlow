# Exercise

## What it is

Physical exercise is any movement that elevates your heart rate. You don't need a gym — bodyweight routines, cycling, swimming, or dancing all count. The goal is to move your body consistently, not perfectly.

## Why it works

Exercise releases endorphins, serotonin, and BDNF (brain-derived neurotrophic factor) — a protein that protects and grows brain cells. Regular exercise reduces symptoms of anxiety and depression as effectively as some medications, improves sleep quality, and builds stress resilience over time.

## How to start

1. Choose something you actually enjoy — the best workout is the one you'll do
2. Start small: 15–20 minutes, 3 times a week
3. Warm up for 5 minutes to prevent injury
4. Track your progress — small wins keep you going

## Types to explore

- **Strength training** — builds muscle and confidence
- **Cardio** — running, cycling, HIIT for heart health
- **Yoga / Pilates** — strength with mindfulness
- **Team sports** — exercise plus social connection

## 2-Minute Version

Do 10 push-ups, 10 squats, and 10 jumping jacks. Set a 2-minute timer and move until it goes off.

*Use this version when you're just getting started. Once the habit feels natural, gradually increase the time.*
