# Breathing Exercises

## What it is
Conscious breathing is the fastest way to shift your nervous system from stress mode to calm mode. Unlike most habits, it works in under 60 seconds — anytime, anywhere.

## Why it works
Slow, controlled breathing activates the vagus nerve, which directly triggers your parasympathetic nervous system. This lowers heart rate, reduces cortisol, and signals safety to your brain. It's the only automatic body function you can consciously control — and that control is a superpower.

## Techniques

### 4-7-8 Breathing
Best for: anxiety, falling asleep
1. Inhale through your nose for **4 seconds**
2. Hold your breath for **7 seconds**
3. Exhale completely through your mouth for **8 seconds**
4. Repeat 3–4 times

### Box Breathing
Best for: stress, before a difficult task
1. Inhale for **4 seconds**
2. Hold for **4 seconds**
3. Exhale for **4 seconds**
4. Hold for **4 seconds**
5. Repeat 4 times

### Physiological Sigh
Best for: immediate stress relief
1. Inhale fully through your nose
2. Take a second, short sniff to fully expand your lungs
3. Long, slow exhale through your mouth
4. Repeat 2–3 times

## 2-Minute Version
Take 3 deep belly breaths right now. Inhale so your stomach expands, not your chest. Exhale fully. That's it — and it works.

## Atomic Habits tip
Practice breathing exercises *before* a stressful event — a presentation, a difficult conversation, an exam. Over time, your nervous system learns to regulate itself faster.
