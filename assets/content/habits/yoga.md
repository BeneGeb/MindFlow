# Yoga

## What it is

Yoga combines physical postures, breath control, and mindfulness into a single practice. It's not about flexibility — it's about the connection between your body and mind.

## Why it works

Yoga activates the parasympathetic nervous system (your "rest and digest" mode), directly countering the stress response. Regular practice reduces cortisol, improves sleep, lowers blood pressure, and significantly reduces symptoms of anxiety and depression.

## How to start

1. You don't need to be flexible — stiffness is exactly why you should start
2. A mat and 20 minutes of floor space are all you need
3. Begin with beginner sequences: Sun Salutations are a great foundation
4. Focus on your breath throughout — it's the anchor of the practice

## Styles to explore

- **Hatha** — slow, gentle, perfect for beginners
- **Vinyasa** — flowing sequences, more dynamic
- **Yin** — long holds, deeply restorative
- **Restorative** — props and passive stretches for recovery

## 2-Minute Version

Child's Pose. Kneel, fold forward, arms extended or alongside your body. Breathe slowly for 2 minutes. You'll feel the difference.
